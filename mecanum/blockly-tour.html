The Blockly screen

What everything is and what it does. Print it: PDF. Next: Programming with Blockly.
  1. 1Robot Whose tabs these are. Leave it on shared. Connecting a robot never changes your tabs.
  2. 2Program tabs Each tab is one program. Click one to open it. Tabs 1 to 9 are also the remote's slots 1 to 9.
  3. 3+ New A blank program, a copy of a demo, an example (like Look first), or the Toolbox of subroutines.
  4. 4This program... Rename, duplicate or delete the open program.
  5. 5Undo, redo Take back the last change (Ctrl+Z), or put it back.
  6. 6RUN Runs the program in the open tab, on the simulator or on the robot.
  7. 7STOP Stops the program and the wheels. Always works.
  8. 8Run on Simulator: the pretend robot on the right. Robot: your real robot, once connected.
  9. 9Serial, Bluetooth Connect to your robot: Serial with a USB cable, Bluetooth without one.
  10. 10Connection Says not connected or how the robot is connected.
  11. 11Sketch Which program is on the robot's Arduino. It picks itself when the robot connects.
  12. 12Remote slots... Turns the program into a remote slot, sends it to the robot, or reads the robot's slots back.
  13. 13Share / phone... A link, a QR code, the program as text or a file. Bring a program in pastes one back.
  14. 14Help What the buttons and blocks do.
  15. 15Categories Click one to see its blocks, then drag a block out onto the workspace.
  16. 16Workspace Build the program here. Only blocks under when RUN is pressed run. Drag empty space to move around.
  17. 17Center, zoom Bring the blocks back into view, make them bigger or smaller.
  18. 18Trash Drag a block here to delete it (or select it and press Delete).
  19. 19Simulator The pretend robot on a floor, 10 cm squares. Drag the robot to choose where it starts.
  20. 20Readout Where the robot is, which way it faces, and what the sonar sees.
  21. 21Map goal What to do on this map. Reach the star to see Made it!
  22. 22Simulator controls Pick a Map, the Speed, the View, a bigger picture, and Back to start.
  23. 23Console, The robot will do, Code What happened, the list of moves your program makes, and the program as code.
  24. 24Console Every move, every say block, and every message from the robot.
  25. 25Command box Type a robot command (like u for the sonar) and press Send. Clear empties the console.

A closer look

The simulator, a block and its menu, and the console.

The simulator

  1. AThe robot Drag it to choose where it starts. RUN and Back to start put it there. The orange arrow is its nose.
  2. BThe star The goal. Reach it and the console says Made it! with your time and bumps.
  3. CWalls The sonar sees them and the robot bumps into them. On some maps they move, or open and close.
  4. DDashed line What the sonar sees: straight ahead, to the nearest wall.
  5. EReadout Position, heading and the sonar reading. Watch it while the program runs.
  6. FMap Ten floors, from easy to hard. From map 4 on, something changes every run.
  7. GSpeed real time to watch closely, faster to finish quickly.
  8. HView The whole floor, or close up around the robot.
  9. IBigger, Full screen A bigger simulator, or the whole screen (Esc to leave).
  10. Jwalls, size The room's walls on or off, and its size in mm.
  11. KBack to start Puts the robot back where it starts (press STOP first).

A block, and its right-click menu

  1. aGear Changes the block: here it adds else if and else. Subroutines use it to add inputs.
  2. bDropdown Click the little triangle to choose: sonar or laser, right or left.
  3. cNumber Click it and type. You can also drop a variable or a math block on it.
  4. dSnap Blocks snap together top to bottom. The ones inside do and else are what if does.
  5. e-kRight-click a block for this menu:
  6. eDuplicate a copy, right next to it
  7. fAdd Comment a note for people (the program ignores it)
  8. gInline Inputs the inputs in one row or stacked
  9. hCollapse Block fold it up small (Expand opens it again)
  10. iDisable Block the program skips it for now
  11. jDelete the block and everything inside it
  12. kHelp about this kind of block

The console

  1. 1plain A message from the page: what it is doing.
  2. 2→ A command the page sent to the robot.
  3. 3← What the robot answered.
  4. 4sim: What the pretend robot did.
  5. 5says: A say block in your program.
  6. 6⚠ A warning: something to look at.
  7. 7✖ An error: the program stopped.
  8. 8★ Made it! You reached the star.
  9. 9The robot will do The moves your program will make, worked out before it runs.
  10. 10Code Your blocks as JavaScript code.
  11. 11Clear Empties the console (or use the clear the console block).
Copy and paste: click a block, Ctrl+C, then Ctrl+V. It works from one tab to another too.
Lost your blocks? Press the center button (17 on page 1), or drag the empty workspace.
Saved by itself: your tabs stay in this browser. To keep a copy, use Share / phone..., Save as a file.
