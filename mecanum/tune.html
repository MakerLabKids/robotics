Tune the robot

ir.ino or programming.ino on the robot

Three quick fixes from a phone: square turns, a true meter, and a straight sideways slide. Each fix is saved on the robot, so it stays after you switch it off.

New to this? How to connect. The red STOP at the bottom of the screen stops the robot at any time. So does any key on the remote.

1. Turn a square corner

  1. Put the robot on the floor with its nose along a line: a floor tile line or a strip of tape.
  2. Press the orange button. The robot turns right four times. That should bring it all the way around, back onto the line.

Where does it point now? Turn the picture until it matches your robot.

2. Drive 1 meter

  1. Mark a start line on the floor, and another line 1 meter (100 cm) away.
  2. Put the robot's front on the start line. It needs clear floor ahead: it stops by itself when something is close in front.
  3. Press the orange button, then measure how far the front went.

3. Slide sideways

To slide, the wheels work in two pairs: the orange pair (front left and back right) and the blue pair (front right and back left). When one pair gets too little power its wheels barely turn, and the slide goes crooked.

  1. Press a slide button and watch the wheels.
  2. Two wheels barely turning? Give their color more power and slide again.
  3. Done when all four wheels work and the robot slides straight sideways.

Did it creep forward or backward while it slid? The page gives a little more to the pair that needs it.
