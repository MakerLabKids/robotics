Programming with Blockly

In the simulator first. Simple things first. Print it: PDF. What every part of the screen is: The Blockly screen. Tricks: Blockly tips and tricks.
Every program starts under when RUN is pressed. Press RUN to try it, STOP to stop it, Back to start to put the robot back. Mistakes are fine: change one thing and run it again.
1Open Blockly

On a computer: robotics.makerlabkids.com/mecanum/blocks. Pick Simulator. The pretend robot is on the right.

2Your first move

Drag drive forward from Moves and snap it under when RUN is pressed. Press RUN.

3Add a turn

Snap turn right 90° under it. Click a number to change it. Run it again.

4Repeat

A square is 4 times "drive, turn". repeat (in Loops) does it for you.

5Pick a map

Under the simulator: Map. Try 2. Star behind the box. Drag the robot to move where it starts.

6Variables

A variable is a box with a number in it. change side by 100 makes each side longer: a spiral.

7Subroutines

Make your own block (Subroutines). The gear adds an input like size.

8Watch a sensor

say puts a number in the Console. Watch the sonar change as the robot moves.

9Repeat until

Drive until something is near, then stop. The wall is somewhere new every run on map 4.

10If ... else

Decide! If the way is blocked, turn. Else, go forward. Try maps 7 and 8.

11Follow the light

light direction says which way the lamp is. Turn that much. Try maps 6 and 9.

12The toolbox

+ New, then Toolbox: subroutines: ready-made blocks like creep to wall. On the real robot: Bluetooth, Robot, RUN.

Ten maps

Pick one under the simulator: Map. The star says when you made it.
Maps 4 to 10 change every run: the wall moves, the door opens and closes, the lamp goes dark. A program that only counts millimeters will miss. Use the sensors and if to decide.
1Square room

Drive a square. Then try the light and the loop of tape.

Use: drive, turn, repeat

2Star behind the box

Get to the star. Go around the box.

Use: drive (slide left / right), turn

3The hallway

Follow the hallway to the star. Plan every move.

Use: drive, turn, a subroutine for each part

4Stop at the wall

The wall moves every run. Stop on the star, closer than 20 cm, no bump.

Use: keep driving, repeat until, sonar sees closer than

5Stop on the dark tile

A different tile is dark every run. Stop on it.

Use: repeat until sees the line, stop

6Find the light

The lamp is in a different corner every run. Find it.

Use: light direction, turn, follow the light

7Find the gap

The gap moves every run. Slide until the sonar sees through it, then go.

Use: keep driving (slide), repeat while, sonar

8The door

Wait until the sonar sees the door open, then go through.

Use: repeat while, wait, sonar, drive

9Red light, green light

Move only while the lamp is on. Moving in the dark gets you caught.

Use: light level, if ... else, wait

10The maze

Look before you go: turn, check the sonar, pick the open way.

Use: subroutines, if, sonar, repeat

Made it? The Console says ★ Made it! with your time, bumps and catches. Run it again: does it still work?
Stuck? Put say blocks in your program to see what the sensor reads, and slow the simulator to real time.
Need a hand? + New, Toolbox: subroutines: copy a ready-made subroutine into your tab (Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V).
