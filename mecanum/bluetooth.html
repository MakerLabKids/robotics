Bluetooth:How to connectDrive with Sticks

Connect with Bluetooth

ir.ino or programming.ino on the robot

The robot's Bluetooth module lets a phone drive it, with two sticks on the screen. The remote keeps working the whole time.

What you need

  • The robot, switched on, with its Bluetooth module plugged in (Wiring: VCC to 5V, GND to GND, TXD to A0, RXD to A1).
  • An Android phone or tablet with the Chrome browser. A Chromebook or a computer with Chrome or Edge works too.
  • Not an iPhone or iPad: Safari and Chrome on those cannot talk to the robot.

Connect

  1. Switch the robot on. The little light on the Bluetooth module blinks: it is waiting for a phone.
  2. On the phone, open Chrome and go to
    robotics.makerlabkids.com/mecanum/sticks
    (or point the camera at the square code). Turn the phone sideways and press Full screen: the sticks get big. Exit brings the page back, and you stay connected.
  3. Press Bluetooth at the top. A list pops up. Tap your robot's name, then Pair.
  4. The red dot turns green and the button says Disconnect. You are connected. Drive!

No PIN and no phone settings are needed this way. The phone may ask to turn on Bluetooth or Location the first time: say yes.

Robot not in the list?

First, show every device. Press Cancel. A new line appears under the buttons: Show every Bluetooth device. Press it and look for your robot's name. The list is longer now: it has everything nearby, not just robots.

Still not there? Pair it first. Some Bluetooth modules only show up after the phone has paired with them.

  1. Open the phone's Settings, then Bluetooth. Find your robot's name (a robot that was never named is called HC-05 or HC-06) and tap it.
  2. It asks for a PIN: 1234. If that is wrong, try 0000.
  3. Go back to the Sticks page and press Serial (not Bluetooth). Tap the robot, then Connect.

On a computer it is the same: pair in the computer's Bluetooth settings, then press Serial.

Drive with Sticks

  • Right stick: which way to go. Up is forward, down is back, left and right slide the robot sideways. In between goes diagonally.
  • Left stick: how fast, and turning. Left and right turn the robot. Up makes everything faster, down makes it slower. All the way down is stop.
  • Lift your thumbs and the robot stops. Touch a stick anywhere near its middle: where your thumb lands becomes the middle.
  • STOP stops it right now.
  • Use both thumbs together: slide and turn at the same time. Only a mecanum robot can do that.

Digital at the top changes the sticks to buttons: the right stick has six slanted directions, the left stick is forward, back and turn, and its four corners pick the speed (25, 50, 75, 100). Analog brings the smooth sticks back.

The robot looks after itself: it will not drive forward into a wall, and if the phone goes quiet or out of range it stops within half a second.

When you are done

Press Disconnect. One phone drives one robot: nobody else can connect to your robot until you let go of it.

If it does not work

It does thisTry this
The Bluetooth and Serial buttons are both grayThis browser cannot do it. Use Chrome on an Android phone, a Chromebook or a computer. iPhones cannot.
The robot is not in the listIs the module's light blinking? If it is dark, check its four wires. If it is on without blinking, another phone is already connected to it. Otherwise press Cancel, then Show every Bluetooth device. Still missing: pair it first and use Serial (above).
Two robots in the list have the same nameThey were never named. Switch the other one off, or connect one robot at a time: a robot that is connected leaves the list.
It says connected but the robot does not moveIs the left stick all the way down? That is stop. Is the robot's battery switch on? Still nothing: the TXD and RXD wires may be swapped (TXD goes to A0, RXD to A1).
It will not go forward, but everything else worksSomething is closer than 25 cm in front of it. Its sonar stops it from hitting a wall.
It stops and says DisconnectedYou walked too far away, or the robot's batteries are low. Come closer and press the button again.
Bluetooth worked before, and now the robot is missing from its listIf the phone was paired with the robot in Settings since then, use Serial, or make the phone forget the robot in Settings.

For the grown-up

  • A robot keeps its name. The name lives inside the Bluetooth module, so uploading a sketch does not change it. A robot that was never named is called HC-05 or HC-06, like every other new module: switch those on one at a time. To name a robot: plug in USB, open the console at the bottom of the Programming page, send kN and the name (for example kNRexy;, up to 16 letters, digits and dashes), then switch the robot off and on.
  • Why a named robot can be missing from the first list. That list shows robots it can recognize, and a phone that has never met a robot's name may not recognize it. "Show every Bluetooth device" lists everything.
  • One command at a time. The module cannot listen while the robot is answering, so the pages wait for each answer and send a stop more than once.
  • From a computer, the Blocks page and the console on the Programming page connect the same way, with the same two buttons.
